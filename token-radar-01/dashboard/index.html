03 / Methodology & evidence limits

Base = TVL × proxy median × momentum × tracking age. The multiple is the median reported market cap / TVL (ratios clipped to 0.05–3×) of at least five priced snapshot peers. Cohorts: same category and within 5× TVL, category, size, then all priced peers; default 0.35×. These are capitalization proxies, not observed FDVs. Momentum is 1 + 7-day TVL change clipped to ±35%; age scales 0.88–1.23× over the first two years tracked. Missing inputs are neutral and widen the band. The low/high scenarios use adjusted cohort quartiles; they are not statistical confidence bounds.

*The official watchlist is always included. Extended candidates are below the watchlist floor and exclude recorded token signals and obvious derivative patterns. Parent relationships are not exposed in the snapshot. A missing record does not prove a token is absent; a recorded symbol does not prove liquid trading. “Stronger” describes peer fit and available data, not launch odds. Farm routes are conditional and fees are illustrative, not quotes. No points or rewards are verified here.
